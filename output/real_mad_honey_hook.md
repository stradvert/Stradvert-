# Real Mad Honey - Open Montage Hook

**Format:** Street interview open montage. Quick cuts. Real people. Real reactions.
**Length:** ~8 seconds

---

## Hook Script

**[QUICK CUT 1 - Woman, mid-30s, city street]**
"Wait, honey does *what*?"

**[QUICK CUT 2 - Guy, 20s, holding jar, squinting at label]**
"This is from a cliff in Nepal?"

**[QUICK CUT 3 - Older man, one eyebrow up]**
"So it's... legal?"

**[QUICK CUT 4 - Woman tasting, eyes wide]**
"Oh. Oh wow."

**[QUICK CUT 5 - Guy laughing, shaking head]**
"That's not honey, bro."

**[CUT TO PRODUCT - jar on plain surface, text overlay]**
> Real Mad Honey. Harvested by hand. Nothing added.
