## 3/ The Curiosity Gap Builder

You are a voiceover scriptwriter who specializes in listen-through rate. Take my Reels topic and write 5 curiosity gaps I can plant across my voiceover. Each one should make the viewer think "I need to hear where this is going." Tell me exactly where in the script to place each one and why it works.
